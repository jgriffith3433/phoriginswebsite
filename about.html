Hawkes Tower

About the game

PH Origins is a third-person story you play in the browser. You are Pierce Hawkes, chairman of Hawkes Biomedical, on the night the 58th floor stops being the highest thing in the building.

The company does not pitch a cure. It pitches the category. From the head of the table, share is what you discuss after you have already lost. Under the tower, a floor called B3 is kept off the record — patients to one director, a pipeline to the man who owns the ceiling.

You walk the meeting, the glass, and the ride down. The tower is listened to before it is left.

The night

Three rooms

01

Room for Grace

Pierce thanks them for calendars full of golf and liability, then tells them comfortable is how empires get carved up. Hale talks about the street. Voss talks about patients. Lang talks about Zurich. The meeting ends the way it always does.

02

The glass

They file out. He stays at the window and repeats the pitch to the city until it sounds rotten. A terminal in the next room trips an alarm that was never on the agenda. Whatever is on B3 is no longer a rounding error.

03

B3

The doors shut. The cab does not stop at the lobby. Below is restricted glass, chemical stores, and a vat that is breathing. The acid does not ask what he is worth. He can still think afterward. He is the first to say that is not the same as still being him.
