# Chapter openings

Each level opens on a 4-6 s in-engine camera move over its place with the title typeset over it (`Chapter N`, the level name, one story line), a short sting, then a glide into the player's camera (AGENTS.md "Chapter openings"). Shots as built: 1 the 58th-floor hall, rising and tracking toward the board room; 2 out of the B3 cab into the lit hall; 3 the empty over-lit lab; 4 the hall bathroom, pushing toward the mirror wall; 5 the tower lobby; 6 down the station stairs onto the platform; 7 the car, a pan from the far end; 8 the apartment room. Not yet in the shots (the places are not rendered): the Apex exterior climb, the B3 grating, the desk lamp from above, the rainy street, the train arriving, dawn on the building. The Level Complete card closes it
(its eyebrow reads `Chapter N · <name>`, the body is the existing `ui.complete.*` line). Copy is in `locales/en.json`
as `chapter.N.title` and `chapter.N.line` (type `ui`, silent). Lines stay in the game's terse voice and never name
B3, the vat or the creature outright.

1. The Apex Peak — Fifty-eight floors up, the board is seated. Pierce is late.
2. B3 — Below the lobby, the clearance badge still works.
3. The Return — The vat is behind him. The lights are too bright.
4. The Desk — The work is still there. So is the face in the mirror.
5. Going Home — Fifty-eight floors down, the street does not know him yet.
6. The Station — Hawkes Street. The last stairs down.
7. The Train — The uptown 6. Somebody is always one car ahead.
8. The Morning — Three blocks. One room. Morning comes anyway.
