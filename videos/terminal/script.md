Night. Hawkes Tower, 58th floor. Pierce Hawkes, the Chairman, stands at his office window looking out over the city, the boardroom behind him dark and empty.
PIERCE (to himself, quiet): Compliance doesn't set the ceiling. I do.
(His phone buzzes on the desk. VOSS. He lets it ring out. A beat. It buzzes once more: a text.)
TEXT ON SCREEN: Pick up the terminal.
(He turns. On the desk monitor, an alarm blinks red: B3. A security feed. Something moves in the corridor. Not a person. A shape.)
PIERCE: ...Not tonight.
(The elevator. He rides it down alone, floor numbers falling: 58... 30... 1... B1... B3. The doors open on green emergency light. Somewhere in the dark, something wet moves.)
(Cut: the acid vat's glass, a hairline crack running across it. The crack spreads. Pierce's reflection in the glass.)
(Glass bursts. Green light floods everything. Black.)
(In the dark, his voice, the same voice, close:)
PIERCE: I can still think. That is not the same as still being me.
