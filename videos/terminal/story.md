# Pick Up the Terminal (one night in Hawkes Tower)

**Logline:** A chairman who believes nothing below his tower moves without his say is summoned to the one place he swore stayed dark, goes anyway, and finds out what that costs him.
**Pierce:** wants to prove the dark is his to keep; certain, then not. **Voss** (never seen): the call he refuses to take, then the text that makes him go.
**The turn:** the text, "Pick up the terminal." Pierce stops saying no. **Payoff:** the vat bursts in green light and sound; his voice returns in the dark, quieter: thinking is not the same as being himself.
**Why it matters:** the game's premise in 40 s: corporate certainty meets B3; the creature is never shown or named.
**For a stranger:** a powerful man, an ignored call, an alarm, a descent, and a change we hear rather than see.
