# Story: They Locked Him on B3

## Logline
Chairman Pierce Hawkes discovers that the restricted B3 laboratory experiment has broken out of its glass containment vat, forcing him to witness and endure the terrifying biological consequence of corporate ambition.

## Character Wants & Attitudes
- **Pierce Hawkes**: Wants total control and category ownership; views B3 as a pipeline and valuation driver. Attitude: cold, uncompromising, supreme confidence until reality shatters.
- **The Board (Hale, Voss, Lang)**: Want financial valuation, risk mitigation, and Zurich compliance. Attitude: anxious, detached, wary of catastrophe.
- **The Creature (Unnamed)**: Wants absolute freedom. Attitude: primal, overwhelming force that destroys containment.

## The Turn & Payoff
- **The Turn**: Descending to basement B3 via the flashing red emergency elevator, Pierce finds the massive glass containment vat shattered from the inside, fluid pouring out, and empty restraints swinging.
- **The Payoff**: Acid and biological exposure hit Pierce, altering his physical form while retaining his conscious mind. He stares into an office mirror with glowing eyes—proving the dark was never his to promise.

## Why It Matters
It dramatizes the core premise of PH Origins: corporate hubris meeting biological reality at Hawkes Tower.

## Who It's For
Anyone discovering PH Origins for the first time—establishing the mood, stakes, setting (Hawkes Tower 58F to B3), and psychological thriller horror.
