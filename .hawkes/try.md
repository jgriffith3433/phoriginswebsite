1. Open the PH Origins beta, tap to play. The title card rises in over a slowly drifting, dark tower backdrop.
2. Hover or Tab/arrow through Start, Reset, Load Level and Settings: each lights up and ticks softly; press feels pressed.
3. Open Load Level: the Mission Select rows match the card; Back returns.
4. Resize to a phone width (or rotate it): buttons stay full-width and thumb-sized. Press Start to play as before.
