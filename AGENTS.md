## Hard constraints (do not regress)

- **GLB-only** in this website. Convert FBX with Blender tools, then reference the GLB.
- **Do not reintroduce Mixamo JSON retarget / mixamorig rewrite.** That caused T-pose from bone-name collisions. Bake clips in Blender onto `mixamorigN`, load GLB, `glTF` `animationStartMode` none, stop clips without resetting every group to the last pistol pose.
- **Combat off** on Apex Peak and B3 (`combat: false` in `levels/level-library.json`). Do not spawn wave enemies there.
- **Level 3** (`the-return`) reuses the B3 scene, then the Apex Peak scene. Combat stays off. Powers are not in yet.
- Unlock audio on first gesture (`src/game/audioUnlock.ts`). Mobile needs this.
- **Tap to play, Pause, Settings** (studio sound standard). `game.html` opens on a full-screen **Tap to play** prompt (`#tapToPlay`); that tap resumes the AudioContext and primes the pool. If the tap beats `main.ts`, the inline script starts the context and `audioUnlock.ts` adopts it (`window.__phTap.ctx`). The context resumes again on `visibilitychange`, `pageshow`, `focus` and its own `statechange` (iOS "interrupted"). **Settings** (title card and Pause) has Sound on/off, Music and Effects & voice, saved in localStorage `hawkes.sound` as `{ on, music, sfx }` (0-1, `src/game/soundSettings.ts`). Music is the trigger beds and the ending songs (`createUnlockedAudio(url, 'music')`); everything else is effects. Every clip goes through `createUnlockedAudio` (or `trackAudio` for a clone), and Web Audio sounds connect to `busInput(ctx, 'sfx')`, not `ctx.destination`. A tracked clip's `volume` and `muted` read back what the caller set; what plays is scaled by Settings. iOS ignores `volume`, so there a clip between full and silent is routed through a gain node. **Pause** (the ❚❚ button, **Esc**, or leaving the tab) holds the render loop, Babylon animations and every playing clip; `setTimeout` story timers are not held.
- **Clips play through Web Audio** (`sfxMix.ts` `playClip`: decoded buffers cached per URL, about 48 MB, through `busInput`). One-shots, cutscene lines and beds, phone voices and tones, the walk loop and the alarm use it, so a phone hears them without a finger on the screen. Only music beds (`triggers.ts`) and ending songs stay on `<audio>` elements, and those go back to the gesture-primed pool with `releaseUnlockedAudio` when they stop. Do not clone `<audio>` elements per clip: iOS will not start a clone outside a touch. Pause holds Web Audio clips with `holdClips` / `releaseClips` next to `holdAllAudio`.
- **Level unload frees what the level made.** A GLB root's dispose takes its clips, skeletons, materials and textures (`modelLoader.ts` `releaseWithParent`); `disposeSceneNodes` (sceneData.ts) frees per-asset materials and their texture clones; the shared probe and the material catalog cache stay. Level loads run one at a time (`loadMissionScene` chain). `npm run check:leaks` (Node, NullEngine, no browser) reloads a level file and fails if counts grow; run it after touching loaders or materials.
- **Phones and tablets get the low-power graphics tier**: no MSAA, no preserved drawing buffer, no SSAO, a smaller bloom, render size capped near 1 MP. `?quality=high|low` overrides. A lost WebGL context shows the load-error card (`graphics`) with Retry.
- Invert mouse Y is intentional (`thirdPersonCamera.ts`).
- Start stays disabled until `gameReady` (player + NPCs `whenReady`, skybox faces, and a few rendered frames). The black veil stays up through that. Start holds it across the camera switch, waits **1s**, then fades clear over **1.5s**, so the night-city cube does not flash. Characters stay hidden until idle/sit is playing — avoids T-pose on early Start.

## Story play (do not regress)

Levels 1 through 7 are playable: Apex Peak, B3, The Return, The Desk, Going Home, The Station, The Train. Combat stays off on all of them.

**Story copy** lives in `locales/en.json`. English is the default. Each line has a stable `id`, a `type`, a `speaker`, the `text`, an `audio` path, an optional `audioText`, and an editor-only `note`. Spoken types are `phone`, `dialogue`, `bark`, and `shout`. An empty `audio` on those is a missing clip. `text`, `objective`, `hud`, `item`, `credit`, and `ui` stay silent. Phone scripts and cutscene events keep timing and point at a line id. Dev Tools → **Story lines** lists them. Double-click a line to edit it. **Speaker** is the name on the subtitle. **Voice** is the Eleven v4 voice that character uses, saved in `locales/voices.json`, and the dropdown is the account's voice library. **Preview** plays the clip on that line. **Generate** on a row, or **Generate missing / out of date**, calls ElevenLabs and writes an ogg. The ogg stores the sentence, voice id, and performance in a `PHMETA` comment (`audioText`). Older wavs still read their `phln` chunk. A spoken line with a file and no matching record is out of date, which is every clip made before that record existed. Changing a character's voice marks their lines out of date. Takes use **eleven_v4**. Performance defaults to Default, which sends the sentence with no audio tag. Any other choice prefixes that tag, and the ogg records which one was used. ElevenLabs runs through Hawkes Gen (`tools/hawkesgen.mjs` → `C:\Projects\hawkesgen`), which holds the key: start `hawkesgen\auto-start.bat` first. Reload the game after a save. `node tools/extract_locale.mjs` rebuilds ids from the timelines without wiping lines that are already in the catalog. Change a sentence there, not in a cutscene or in `phone.json`.

**Sound effects** live in `locales/sfx.json`. Dev Tools → **Sound effects** lists them. Each effect has an `id`, a `group`, the `text` (the prompt), an `audio` path, an optional `loop`, an optional `duration` in seconds, an `influence`, a `volume`, and an editor-only `note`. Volume is the playback level. Preview plays at that level. The game scales its existing level by volume against the level that effect shipped at, so leaving the slider alone does not change the mix. Volume is not part of the out-of-date check. The game still plays the path in code. The catalog points at that file, and Generate overwrites it. A missing file is **Needs clip**. A file with no record, or a description, loop, duration, or influence that does not match the record, is **Out of date**. **Generate** on a row, **Generate missing / out of date**, and **Regenerate all** call ElevenLabs sound generation (`eleven_text_to_sound_v2`). One description is one sound. Duration is left blank on a hit so the model picks the length. Loop and a duration are set only on a bed the game repeats. Influence is how tightly the sound follows the description. The take is 44.1 kHz, written as the file the game already plays. A wav stores the record in an `sfxm` chunk. An ogg stores it in an `SFXMETA` comment. ElevenLabs runs through Hawkes Gen (`tools/hawkesgen.mjs` → `C:\Projects\hawkesgen`), which holds the key: start `hawkesgen\auto-start.bat` first. Do not generate the whole list unless that is the job. The long beds cost more because their duration is set.

**Missions** live in `src/game/missions.ts`. **O** opens the panel on the bottom left and closes inventory, the same way **I** closes Missions. The list is the live main objective plus the side quests, titled `Missions n/m`. Completion is the `missions` field on the `ph-origins-save` cookie: `Record<libraryId, questId[]>`. New Game sets that to `{}`. Reset on the title clears the cookie. The death screen offers Start (retry this level) and Main Menu (title, progress kept). A quest id that is not in `missionsFor` for the current library id is ignored.

- Apex Peak, B3, The Return, and The Desk: `call-voss` and `call-hale`. A call counts in `phone.ts` `onConnected`, when the call connects, not on no answer.
- Going Home: `power-shadow` (**C** turning it on), `power-shock`, `power-possess`, `power-consume`. Each counts only when that action succeeds.
- The Station is the main objective only.
- The Train (level 7): `call-hale`, answering Hale at the stop (or dialing her there).
- The main quest id is `main`. It is marked in `presentLevelEnd`, when **End Level** is confirmed, so the Level Complete card includes the finish. Load Level rows show `reward · n/m`. The card line is `Missions n/m`.

**End Level is not a walk-up.** `finishLevel` in `src/story/act1.ts` returns unless `which` matches `isApex`, `isB3`, or `isReturn`. `stepWorld` and the board-seat setup run only on those three. `pendingEnd` stores `levelId`, and the **End Level** button shows only when that id is `state.level`. The Return phase `mirror-look` is the look that ends level 3. The Desk starts with its own **Look in mirror**, which calls `theDesk.beginLook`. That scene must not call `finishLevel('return')`. The Desk offers End Level only when its own scene finishes.

`offerEnd` leaves the world visible. The action button reads **End Level**, and Pierce stays put: no walk, jump, or shadow run. That press silences the phone, stops play, plays `ending-{level}`, fades to black, and starts the credit crawl. The card then offers **Continue** and **Exit**. Do not fade or start the song before that press.

**Street and station click.** **V** is not a power key. The current ability is shown at the bottom of the screen. **Tab** switches it, and so does the **Switch Ability** button on the right. With Shadow mode off the two abilities are **Punch** and **Kick** (`Punch` and `Kick` on `ch44-hero`). Click plays that clip and hits whoever is in front; a drawn sidearm still fires instead. With Shadow mode on the two abilities are **Shadow Shock** and **Possess**. The Tab key cap is hidden on mobile. **Tab** always `preventDefault`s and blurs the focused element so it does not highlight page controls. Switching off Possess releases a link. Possess also ends when its **30s** bar empties. Holding **Shift** in Shadow mode is shadow running (`SHADOW_RUN_SECONDS`, **10**): the body drops to a black smoke trail that sits a little behind him and pitches with the climb and the drop, speed is **3×** the old shadow sprint (`SHADOW_RUN_SPEED`), and **Space** flies instead of jumping. A jump and then a second **Space** (or the jump button), held while still in the air, enters that same run: the hold keeps it on and climbing, and releasing it drops out unless **Shift** is still held. The run bar in the ability readout drains, then refills once Shift and that second press are both released. Emptying it locks the run until Shift comes up. A possess link does not shadow-run. Shadow Shock and Possess only work while Shadow mode is on. Click then uses the selected ability: Shadow Shock, or Possess, and a second click kills while linked. Holding the button does not repeat that (`shadowClickEdge`). With Shadow mode off, click is Punch or Kick unless the sidearm is drawn. Aggressive people (hostile, police) play `Punch` from `NPCPunch.fbx` when they swing. The sidearm stays holstered in Shadow mode, and the gun does not fire while it is on. Turning Shadow mode off releases a link. **C** toggles Shadow mode. **Q** raises the hands in Shadow mode and draws the sidearm when it is off.

**Phone.** `HANDSET_HEIGHT` is **0.13**. The unparented spot starts `BEAM_CLEAR` **0.40** m past the tip. Normalize the finger axis every frame: the bone column is length 0.01, and a 1e-4 length test leaves the origin in the wrist on the walk cycle. The handset (`phoneBody`, `phoneScreen`, `phoneLed`) and the player avatar meshes stay on the spot's exclude list, refreshed as the rig loads. Leave the spot enabled at intensity 0. Do not parent the handset or the spot to a bone that is scaled to 0.01. The handset stays on the right-hand bone.

**Window and feed.** After the board leaves, the phase is `to-window` and **Look outside** starts `apex-window`. The security feed mounts when that monologue ends, then the Voss call. Do not show the feed during the monologue.

Do not click **New Game** while checking the running game. It rewrites health, ammo, medkits, and missions. `?beat=` and **Continue** are the safe entries. Do not regenerate ElevenLabs lines that the free tier already blocked.

- When you change walk-up radii, phase names, objective targets, the street route, or the hunt, check the matching
  branch in `phPlaytest.ts`.

## Props and new levels

The game does not run Blender. A level is `levels/<id>.json`. Each prop is one entry in `assets[]` with `kind: "model"`, an `assetId` from `assets/asset-library.json`, and `x` / `y` / `z` / `rotation.y`. Dev Tools and `/game.html` both `fetch` that file (`cache: no-store`). `npm run dev` serves the repo root, so a refresh shows the file on disk.

Dev Tools keeps the scene it loaded and writes that copy back on Save and on most inspector edits. A tab opened before a JSON edit still has the old hallway. **Reload that tab before clicking anything.** Saving it overwrites `levels/<id>.json`.

Add it to `build_office_dressing.py` (or a sibling script in `tools/`), export a GLB into `assets/models/`, and add an id in `assets/asset-library.json` (`path` like `/assets/models/name.glb`). ### New level

1. Add `levels/<id>.json` (`id`, `name`, `theme`, `assets`, `triggers`) and a row in `levels/level-library.json`. Story levels set `combat: false`. Unreleased slots set `comingSoon: true`: Load Level does not list them, Continue does not enter them, and only a `?beat=` or a playtest `setupHidden` starts one.
2. Mirror that row in `FALLBACK_LIBRARY` in `src/game/levels.ts` so a failed library fetch still lists it.
3. Dress it by reusing the ids above before modeling anything new.
4. Reload Dev Tools, then the game. Do not save from a tab that loaded the previous file.

## Known pitfalls

- **T-pose**: clips not ready, wrong clip tail (`PistolIdle` vs `Idle`), glTF autoplaying all groups, Mixamo bone collisions. Gate on `whenReady`; hide mesh until locomotion applied.
- **Invisible pistol**: albedo alpha. Flash without mesh = same bug or `setVisible` timing.
- **WebGL texImage2D / generateMipmap**: disposing a CubeTexture while faces still load, or disposing the shared office probe with a prop (`releaseCarSource` must detach it first). The next level then draws flat grey. `skybox.ts` skips rebuild and disposes the night-city cube once, after its faces are ready. Street and Station share that office probe. Create cubes with `noMipmap`.
- **Walk SFX inaudible**: was `play()` spam while paused. Loop once at ~0.28.
- **Stuck idle**: a one-shot `playClip` (punch, kick, draw, fall) locks cinematic, and `setLocomotion` waits until `resumeLocomotion`. Looping idle, reborn idle, and walk do not lock, so a level start can leave that pose without blocking walk and jump. Do not `playClip` a looping idle after `resumeLocomotion`.
- **City prop hitch**: every placement used to parse its own GLB, and those parses ran back to back on one turn. Static props now parse once per file and later copies instantiate. Each parse waits a frame. Characters still load a fresh container so clips stay on that rig. The editor uses that same loader. Its scene is marked `editor`, so those copies stay pickable and unfrozen and a gizmo still moves the mesh. The game freezes static copies and turns picking off.
- **B3 untextured / dark, `GL_MAX_VERTEX_UNIFORM_BUFFERS (12)`**: glTFLoader raises every material's `maxSimultaneousLights` to `scene.lights.length` (hemi + sun + 23 practicals = 25). Each light is a vertex uniform block. `capSimultaneousLights` in `modelLoader.ts` puts the cap back to 4 after every GLB import. Do not remove that.
- **Phone flashlight**: materials only shade 4 lights, and the scene list is creation order, so a late practical (the office light) never used to get a slot. `phone.ts` rebuilds each mesh's list as fill, sun, then the nearest practicals, with the spot fixed in the last slot. Leave the spot enabled at intensity 0 when the beam is off. Disabling it, or giving it `renderPriority`, swaps a shader slot and the first frame draws with a uniform buffer that is too small (skybox flash, room lights drop out). Do not parent the handset or the spot to the Mixamo bone: its 0.01 scale turns the slab into a wedge, and a parented spot loses the mirrored finger axis and aims backward. The handset is parented to the right-hand bone with a fixed local pose, so the camera does not spin it in the palm. The spot is not parented. It follows the finger axis, pitched slightly down, and its origin sits **0.40** m past the tip of the **0.13** m handset so the slab and the hand do not block the cone. Normalize that finger axis before the offset. The bone column is length 0.01, and a 1e-4 length test treats it as a failed axis, so the spot pops into the wrist while the walk cycle turns the hand. The handset and the player avatar meshes stay on the spot's exclude list (refreshed as the rig loads) and the handset does not receive shadows. A cutscene can aim it at a world point instead. The cone is angle **0.5**, exponent **8**, intensity **30**, range **14** — a tighter beam, with the rim faded before the cutoff.
- Editing `play/cutscenes` or `play/levels` does nothing in `npm run dev`.

## Analytics (Hawkes Analytics)

Hawkes Analytics is the only analytics here (contract: `hawkesgen/docs/analytics.md`, product id `phorigins`).
GoatCounter is gone; never add another tracker. Devtools (`devtools.html`, the level editor) loads none of it.

- **Site pages** (index, about, characters, whatsnew) load `./play/hawkes-build.js` and then
  `https://gen.hawkesgen.ai/sdk/analytics@1.js` with `data-product="phorigins"`, both `defer`, in the head. A new
  site page copies those two tags and the footer's `p.site-privacy` line (anonymous statistics, no cookies, no
  personal data, no IP stored). `site/marketing.js` adds `play_click` (`page`, `where`: nav / page / footer), `video`
  `trailer` play (focus moving into the YouTube frame) and `feature` `site.music`.
- **The game** loads the SDK from Gen too: `game.html` has the `analytics@1.js` tag (`defer`, before the game module).
  Never a copy here (J, 2026-10-27): a Gen deploy updates every site at once. `src/analytics/hawkes-analytics.d.ts` is
  a copy of Gen's types, only for the compiler. If the SDK hasn't loaded, `sdk()` in `src/game/analytics.ts` queues the
  calls in `hawkesAnalyticsQ` (the SDK runs them when it arrives), so the game never depends on Gen. `src/game/analytics.ts`
  (`createGameAnalytics`) inits it and owns every game event; `main.ts` makes it at the end, next to the playtest
  probe, with its own read-only probe, and calls `analytics?.…` at the real moments. Settings says the same privacy
  line (`.settings-privacy`).
- **Build:** `tools/buildInfo.mjs` gives `{ version, commit, date }`: `HAWKES_BUILD_VERSION/_COMMIT/_DATE` from
  `pages.yml`, else `releases.json` `current`, `git rev-parse` and the clock (Studio's beta build). `vite.config.ts`
  defines it as `__HAWKES_BUILD__` (the game sets `window.HAWKES_BUILD`) and writes `play/hawkes-build.js` for the
  site pages.
- **Context:** `mode` story, `graphics` low/high, `level` (1-7), `level_name`, `objective` (the HUD kicker + text),
  `area` (the room on the 58th floor or in B3 from `apexPlan` / `b3Plan` rooms, else the scene theme).
- **Events** (where they are sent):
  - `game_ready` + `screen` title: the boot level loaded and Start works (boot IIFE after `phBoot.ready()`).
  - `level_start`: end of `beginGame` (each attempt; a start on a level still open closes it as `restart`).
    `level_end`: `complete` in `presentLevelEnd` (End Level pressed), `death` with the death, `quit` on Main Menu
    from Defeat or when the tab closes inside a level. Props: `name`, `objective`, `steps`, `area`, `uses` (counts).
  - `death` in `finishGame(false)`: `cause` is the last thing that hurt Pierce (`applyHurt(amount, cause)`:
    `creature`, `police`, `hostile`, `car`, `enemy`, `playtest`), with `area`, `x`, `z`, `objective`.
  - `progress`: each new objective (the watcher reads the HUD once a second): `step` (grows per level), `label`.
  - `stuck` (custom): three minutes of real play (not paused, no scene or card, tab visible) on one objective;
    again at 6 and 9 minutes. `level`, `objective`, `minutes`, `area`, `x`, `z`.
  - `video`: every timeline cutscene (`beginNamedCutscene` play; `endCutscene` complete or skip with `at`, the
    timeline second Skip was pressed) and the scripted scenes (`going-home-lobby`, `the-desk`, `station-opening`,
    seen by the watcher).
  - `feature`: `dialogue.read` / `dialogue.skipped` / `dialogue.declined` for calls (`phone.ts` `onCallEnded`:
    heard / of lines) and cutscene lines; `settings.sound` / `settings.music` / `settings.sfx` (on change, not per
    slider step); the first use per level of `punch`, `kick`, `sidearm`, `shadow-mode`, `shadow-shock`, `possess`,
    `consume`, `flashlight`, `phone`, `medkit`, `missions`, `inventory`; `side_quest`; `new-game`,
    `reset-progress`, `level-select`.
  - `screen`: title, level, pause, settings-title / settings-pause, level-select, level-complete, defeat.
- **Feedback cards** (`ask`, gold on the panel black): after Level Complete on levels 1-6 (thumbs + comment,
  `after-level-N`), after level 7 "What did you think of the story?" (stars + comment, `story-end`), and on the
  Defeat card after the second death on a level ("Is this part fair?"). Never mid-play: a card still up when the next
  level starts is removed.
- No per-frame events: the watcher reads once a second and sends only on change. Nothing personal in props.
- Playtest `analytics` reloads once with `?hawkes-analytics-debug=1`, plays level 1, logs each event from
  `hawkesAnalytics.debugLog` (`analytics:<name>`) with the build and env, and checks the feedback card. When you
  move an objective, a cutscene or a death, keep its event where the moment really is.
