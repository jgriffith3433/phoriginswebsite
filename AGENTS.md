## What this repo is

Third-person web game + landing page + in-browser level editor. **Babylon.js 7**, **Vite**, **TypeScript**. No React, no Unity.

Sibling source-asset repo (FBX, raw textures): `C:\Projects\phoriginsassets`. **Runtime on the website is GLB / WebP / audio only.** Do not check FBX into this repo or serve `.fbx` from Vite.

## Hard constraints (do not regress)

- **GLB-only** in this website. Convert FBX with Blender tools, then reference the GLB.
- **Do not reintroduce Mixamo JSON retarget / mixamorig rewrite.** That caused T-pose from bone-name collisions. Bake clips in Blender onto `mixamorigN`, load GLB, `glTF` `animationStartMode` none, stop clips without resetting every group to the last pistol pose.
- **Combat off** on Apex Peak and B3 (`combat: false` in `levels/level-library.json`). Do not spawn wave enemies there.
- **Level 3** (`the-return`) reuses the B3 scene, then the Apex Peak scene. Combat stays off. Powers are not in yet.
- Unlock audio on first gesture (`src/game/audioUnlock.ts`). Mobile needs this.
- Invert mouse Y is intentional (`thirdPersonCamera.ts`).
- Start stays disabled until `gameReady` (player + NPCs `whenReady`, skybox faces, and a few rendered frames). The black veil stays up through that. Start holds it across the camera switch, waits **1s**, then fades clear over **1.5s**, so the night-city cube does not flash. Characters stay hidden until idle/sit is playing — avoids T-pose on early Start.

## Story play (do not regress)

Levels 1 through 6 are playable: Apex Peak, B3, The Return, The Desk, Going Home, The Station. Combat stays off on all of them.

**Missions** live in `src/game/missions.ts`. **O** opens the panel on the bottom left and closes inventory, the same way **I** closes Missions. The list is the live main objective plus the side quests, titled `Missions n/m`. Completion is the `missions` field on the `ph-origins-save` cookie: `Record<libraryId, questId[]>`. New Game sets that to `{}`. Reset on the title clears the cookie. The death screen offers Start (retry this level) and Main Menu (title, progress kept). A quest id that is not in `missionsFor` for the current library id is ignored.

- Apex Peak, B3, The Return, and The Desk: `call-voss` and `call-hale`. A call counts in `phone.ts` `onConnected`, when the call connects, not on no answer.
- Going Home: `power-shadow` (**C** turning it on), `power-shock`, `power-possess`, `power-consume`. Each counts only when that action succeeds.
- The Station is the main objective only.
- The main quest id is `main`. It is marked in `presentLevelEnd`, when **End Level** is confirmed, so the Level Complete card includes the finish. Load Level rows show `reward · n/m`. The card line is `Missions n/m`.

**End Level is not a walk-up.** `finishLevel` in `src/story/act1.ts` returns unless `which` matches `isApex`, `isB3`, or `isReturn`. `stepWorld` and the board-seat setup run only on those three. `pendingEnd` stores `levelId`, and the **End Level** button shows only when that id is `state.level`. The Return phase `mirror-look` is the look that ends level 3. The Desk starts with its own **Look in mirror**, which calls `theDesk.beginLook`. That scene must not call `finishLevel('return')`. The Desk offers End Level only when its own scene finishes.

`offerEnd` leaves the world visible. The action button reads **End Level**. That press silences the phone, stops play, plays `ending-{level}`, fades to black, and starts the credit crawl. The card then offers **Continue** and **Exit**. Do not fade or start the song before that press.

**Street and station click.** **V** is not a power key. The current ability is shown at the bottom of the screen. **Tab** switches it, and so does the **Switch Ability** button on the right. With Shadow mode off the two abilities are **Punch** and **Kick** (`Punch` and `Kick` on `ch44-hero`). Click plays that clip and hits whoever is in front; a drawn sidearm still fires instead. With Shadow mode on the two abilities are **Shadow Shock** and **Possess**. The Tab key cap is hidden on mobile. **Tab** always `preventDefault`s and blurs the focused element so it does not highlight page controls. Switching off Possess releases a link. Possess also ends when its **30s** bar empties. Holding **Shift** in Shadow mode is shadow running (`SHADOW_RUN_SECONDS`, **10**): the body drops to a black smoke trail that sits a little behind him and pitches with the climb and the drop, speed is **3×** the old shadow sprint (`SHADOW_RUN_SPEED`), and **Space** flies instead of jumping. A jump and then a second **Space** (or the jump button), held while still in the air, enters that same run: the hold keeps it on and climbing, and releasing it drops out unless **Shift** is still held. The run bar in the ability readout drains, then refills once Shift and that second press are both released. Emptying it locks the run until Shift comes up. A possess link does not shadow-run. Shadow Shock and Possess only work while Shadow mode is on. Click then uses the selected ability: Shadow Shock, or Possess, and a second click kills while linked. Holding the button does not repeat that (`shadowClickEdge`). With Shadow mode off, click is Punch or Kick unless the sidearm is drawn. Aggressive people (hostile, police) play `Punch` from `NPCPunch.fbx` when they swing. The sidearm stays holstered in Shadow mode, and the gun does not fire while it is on. Turning Shadow mode off releases a link. **C** toggles Shadow mode. **Q** raises the hands in Shadow mode and draws the sidearm when it is off.

**Phone.** `HANDSET_HEIGHT` is **0.13**. The unparented spot starts `BEAM_CLEAR` **0.40** m past the tip. Normalize the finger axis every frame: the bone column is length 0.01, and a 1e-4 length test leaves the origin in the wrist on the walk cycle. The handset (`phoneBody`, `phoneScreen`, `phoneLed`) and the player avatar meshes stay on the spot's exclude list, refreshed as the rig loads. Leave the spot enabled at intensity 0. Do not parent the handset or the spot to a bone that is scaled to 0.01. The handset stays on the right-hand bone.

**Window and feed.** After the board leaves, the phase is `to-window` and **Look outside** starts `apex-window`. The security feed mounts when that monologue ends, then the Voss call. Do not show the feed during the monologue.

Do not click **New Game** while checking the running game. It rewrites health, ammo, medkits, and missions. `?beat=` and **Continue** are the safe entries. Do not regenerate ElevenLabs lines that the free tier already blocked.

## Known pitfalls

- **T-pose**: clips not ready, wrong clip tail (`PistolIdle` vs `Idle`), glTF autoplaying all groups, Mixamo bone collisions. Gate on `whenReady`; hide mesh until locomotion applied.
- **Invisible pistol**: albedo alpha. Flash without mesh = same bug or `setVisible` timing.
- **WebGL texImage2D / generateMipmap**: disposing a CubeTexture while faces still load, or disposing the shared office probe with a prop (`releaseCarSource` must detach it first). The next level then draws flat grey. `skybox.ts` skips rebuild and disposes the night-city cube once, after its faces are ready. Street and Station share that office probe. Create cubes with `noMipmap`.
- **Walk SFX inaudible**: was `play()` spam while paused. Loop once at ~0.28.
- **Stuck idle**: `playClip` locks cinematic by default, and `setLocomotion` does nothing while that flag is set, so the capsule moves and the idle clip keeps looping. `resumeLocomotion` clears it. A looping `playClip` after that resume locks it again. Punch and Kick clear it because their end callback resumes. The station handoff and The Desk finish only resume.
- **City prop hitch**: every placement used to parse its own GLB, and those parses ran back to back on one turn. Static props now parse once per file and later copies instantiate. Each parse waits a frame. Characters still load a fresh container so clips stay on that rig. The editor uses that same loader. Its scene is marked `editor`, so those copies stay pickable and unfrozen and a gizmo still moves the mesh. The game freezes static copies and turns picking off.
- **B3 untextured / dark, `GL_MAX_VERTEX_UNIFORM_BUFFERS (12)`**: glTFLoader raises every material's `maxSimultaneousLights` to `scene.lights.length` (hemi + sun + 23 practicals = 25). Each light is a vertex uniform block. `capSimultaneousLights` in `modelLoader.ts` puts the cap back to 4 after every GLB import. Do not remove that.
- **Phone flashlight**: materials only shade 4 lights, and the scene list is creation order, so a late practical (the office light) never used to get a slot. `phone.ts` rebuilds each mesh's list as fill, sun, then the nearest practicals, with the spot fixed in the last slot. Leave the spot enabled at intensity 0 when the beam is off. Disabling it, or giving it `renderPriority`, swaps a shader slot and the first frame draws with a uniform buffer that is too small (skybox flash, room lights drop out). Do not parent the handset or the spot to the Mixamo bone: its 0.01 scale turns the slab into a wedge, and a parented spot loses the mirrored finger axis and aims backward. The handset is parented to the right-hand bone with a fixed local pose, so the camera does not spin it in the palm. The spot is not parented. It follows the finger axis, pitched slightly down, and its origin sits **0.40** m past the tip of the **0.13** m handset so the slab and the hand do not block the cone. Normalize that finger axis before the offset. The bone column is length 0.01, and a 1e-4 length test treats it as a failed axis, so the spot pops into the wrist while the walk cycle turns the hand. The handset and the player avatar meshes stay on the spot's exclude list (refreshed as the rig loads) and the handset does not receive shadows. A cutscene can aim it at a world point instead. The cone is angle **0.5**, exponent **8**, intensity **30**, range **14** — a tighter beam, with the rim faded before the cutoff.
