Chapter 6 · Hawkes Street, platform level

The Station

A man on the platform looks a lot like him. The doors of the uptown train are open.

The platform smells like rain on iron. A commuter waits by the west car. Beside him stands a darker copy of the same man, and a moment later it is gone.

Another figure steps off the train and talks to the commuter about uptown, this car, saving a seat. It has Pierce’s coat and Pierce’s walk and a face that has not changed. Pierce asks the one question that matters: how is he talking to him if he is standing here?

Hale calls to say the board went well. The door is still open. It is the only way forward.
